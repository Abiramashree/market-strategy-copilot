# Case study: Brand-campaign sentiment & backlash (Coca-Cola, Jaguar, BMW)

Domain: Marketing / brand strategy + NLP. Source project: Campaign_analysis.

## Business situation
Three high-profile brand moves drew mixed-to-negative public reaction. The goal was to
measure sentiment and emotion around each, learn what drove backlash vs acceptance, and
build a forward **action plan for Jaguar's** marketing.

## Method
- Collected news/press coverage per campaign; ran **sentiment** (positive / neutral /
  negative with scores) and **emotion** classification (joy, anger, sadness, …), plus
  per-emotion **bigram frequencies** to see what people actually talked about.
- Reported in three Tableau dashboards (one per brand).

## The three campaigns
1. **Coca-Cola — AI-generated Christmas ad.** Backlash centred on using generative AI for
   a nostalgic, human holiday brand; critics saw it as cost-cutting that cheapened the
   emotional equity Coke's Christmas advertising is famous for.
2. **Jaguar — full rebrand for the all-electric transition** ("Exuberant Modernism": bold,
   unexpected, original). Coverage volume was high; sentiment skewed **neutral with pockets
   of joy and vocal anger**. Dominant talking points (bigrams) were "jaguar rebrand" and
   "new logo" — i.e. the conversation was about the *identity change itself*, not the cars.
3. **BMW — 2020 flat/transparent logo redesign** (first change since 1997). Reaction was
   **mostly neutral** — a quieter, design-press story that normalised quickly.

## Key findings
- Backlash scales with **perceived distance from brand heritage** and with **how much the
  change looks like cost-cutting rather than customer value** (Coke AI ad, Jaguar's abrupt
  identity shift) — BMW's incremental logo tweak barely moved sentiment.
- For Jaguar, the noise was concentrated on the **logo/identity**, overshadowing the
  actual EV product story the rebrand was meant to launch.

## Strategy implications (action plan for Jaguar)
- **Reconnect the rebrand to product substance:** push the EV lineup and proof points so
  the story is about the cars, not just the wordmark.
- **Acknowledge heritage** while explaining the EV rationale, to blunt the "betrayed the
  brand" narrative.
- **Monitor sentiment continuously** (not one-shot) and respond to the specific
  anger-driving bigrams; convert high-joy moments into amplified content.
